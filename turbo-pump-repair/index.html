Turbo pump repair for maglev and lubricated turbomolecular pumps

Trotec Solutions repairs magnetically levitated and lubricated bearing turbomolecular pumps from the major OEMs in our Tempe shop. Every repair is tested, documented under the pump's serial number and covered by a 12-month warranty.

What turbo pump repair includes

Each pump goes through test and evaluation before any work, so the quote matches what the pump actually needs. Our capabilities include:

  • Standard service and major service
  • Test and evaluation, and failure analysis
  • Reconfiguration
  • Exchange program while your pump is repaired
  • Set matching: pump, controller and cable tuned together

Our technicians train continuously on the latest balancing, vibration analysis and diagnostic equipment, and our service procedures were developed by people with years of field experience.

How a turbo pump repair works, step by step

The same sequence for every pump, recorded under its serial number.

  1. Send the model and serial number

    Request a quote with the manufacturer, model, serial and part number so we can check its history and parts.

  2. Ship your pump to our Tempe shop

    Send the pump, and the controller and cable if you have them, so we can test and tune them together.

  3. Test, evaluation and failure analysis

    We find what failed and why, then confirm the repair scope with you before work starts.

  4. Repair, balance and run test

    Standard or major service, bearing and rotor work and controller tuning, documented step by step.

  5. Return with a 12-month warranty

    Your pump ships back covered against part defects and workmanship for 12 months.

Common questions

What's the difference between standard and major service?

Standard service restores a running pump: inspection, bearing work where needed and a full test. Major service covers pumps with deeper wear or damage. We confirm which one your pump needs after test and evaluation.

Can you repair a turbo pump that crashed?

Often, yes. Failure analysis shows what was damaged and why, and we quote the repair before any work starts so you can decide between repair and an exchange unit.

Do you repair older or discontinued models?

Yes. Many of the pumps we see are legacy units the OEM no longer supports. Send the model and serial number and we'll confirm.

How is the repair documented?

Each step is digitally documented and kept in our database under the pump's serial number, so the next service starts with its full history.
