Common questions

What's included in a cryopump overhaul?

We offer standard service and major overhaul. Both start with test and evaluation and use OEM components, and every step is documented under the pump's serial number.

Do you offer exchange cryopumps?

Yes. Our exchange program covers cryopumps, so a rebuilt unit can go on your tool while yours is serviced.

Can you service cryopumps shipped from outside the US?

Yes. We receive pumps from customers around the world. Request a quote before you ship, and we'll confirm the paperwork your shipment needs.
