Exchange or repair: which fits your downtime

A standard repair sends your pump to our shop and back. An exchange puts a rebuilt pump on your tool first, so the tool returns to production while your pump goes through repair.

  • Repair: best when the tool can wait or you have a spare.
  • Exchange: best when the tool is down and every day counts.
  • Seed stock: fabs on a fab support program can keep exchange units on hand.

Common questions

How do I ask for an exchange unit?

Check the exchange box on the quote form, or call us. We'll tell you whether a rebuilt pump for your tool is in stock.

Does an exchange pump have a warranty?

Yes. Exchange and rebuilt pumps are covered by the same 12-month warranty against part defects and workmanship.

Do you have exchange cryopumps too?

The exchange program covers cryopumps as well as turbo pumps. Ask with your quote request.
