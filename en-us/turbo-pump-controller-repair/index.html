Why we test the pump, controller and cable together

Because the electronics work is done in-house, our technicians troubleshoot the pump and controller side by side and match them as a complete set. For maglev pumps this matters most; see maglev turbo pump repair.

Common questions

Can you repair a controller the OEM no longer supports?

Yes. We service legacy controllers as well as current integrated systems. Send the controller model and serial number to confirm.

Do I need to send the pump with the controller?

It helps. With the pump, controller and cable together, we can repair, then match and tune the complete set.

Which controller faults do you fix?

Controller software problems, levitation faults, turbo spin-up issues and power failures, along with power supplies and other electronic peripherals.
