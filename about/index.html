About us

An independent, owner-run vacuum pump repair shop

Trotec Solutions is a full-service facility for ultra-high vacuum pumps and ancillaries, serving customers around the world from our shop in Tempe, Arizona. Our goal is to minimize downtime, maximize uptime and extend the life of your equipment.

Why fabs and labs choose an independent shop

Our customers count on OEM-level support and realistic turn-times without OEM-level pricing. All services are performed in-house by technicians and engineers who specialize in ultra-high vacuum pumps and ancillaries.

We build partnerships with our customers so we understand the challenges they face with turbomolecular pumps, controllers and cryopumps. Every repair is documented, and every repair carries a 12-month warranty.
