How a repair moves through the shop

From quote request to a documented, warrantied pump

The same steps for every pump and controller, recorded in our database under its serial number.

  1. Send the model and serial number

    Request a quote with the manufacturer, model, serial and part number so we can check its history and parts.

  2. Ship your pump to our Tempe shop

    Send the pump, and the controller and cable if you have them, so we can test and tune them together.

  3. Test, evaluation and failure analysis

    We find what failed and why, then confirm the repair scope with you before work starts.

  4. Repair, balance and run test

    Standard or major service, bearing and rotor work and controller tuning, documented step by step.

  5. Return with a 12-month warranty

    Your pump ships back covered against part defects and workmanship for 12 months.

Before you send a pump

Should I send the controller with the pump?

Yes, when you can. We test the pump, controller and cable as a set and tune them to each other, which is how most levitation and spin-up faults get traced.

What does the 12-month warranty cover?

Every turbo pump, cryopump, controller and fab support repair is covered for 12 months against part defects and workmanship.

Can I get a replacement pump while mine is repaired?

Ask for an exchange unit with your quote request. If a rebuilt pump for your tool is in stock, it can ship while yours is in the shop.

Do you accept pumps from outside the United States?

Yes. We receive pumps from customers around the world. Request a quote before you ship, and we'll confirm the paperwork your shipment needs.

Do you repair pumps from any manufacturer?

We repair the turbo pump brands on our line card, in both maglev and lubricated bearing designs. For anything else, send the model and serial number and we'll confirm.
